# ScopeGuard Pro — User Guide & Playbook

## How to Protect Your Agency Revenue in 3 Steps:

### 1\. Configure the Extra Request

When a client requests a feature or revision outside the original SOW:

- Enter your hourly rate, estimated hours, and contingency buffer.  
- Select rush speed (1.0x standard, 1.25x expedited, 1.5x emergency).  
- Describe the scope and itemize deliverables.

### 2\. Copy the Communication Script

Switch to the **Negotiation & Email Scripts** tab to copy pre-formatted responses:

- **Diplomatic**: Best for regular, cooperative clients.  
- **Formal**: Best for enterprise corporate procurement.  
- **Retainer Pivot**: Best for upselling ongoing maintenance contracts.

### 3\. Print / Save Legal Change Order

Click **Print / Save as PDF** to generate a clean, signed contract addendum for mutual sign-off before commencing work.
